Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 19, 2026

1. Overview

Money Tree ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance management application that helps you track your income, expenses, budgets, and financial data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Money Tree operates entirely on your device:

  • Local Storage: All your financial data is stored locally on your device using your browser's storage
  • No Server Collection: We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your financial data on our servers
  • Web Analytics: The app uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics (page views, session duration, referrer). This is privacy-friendly and does not use cookies or track individual users. No financial data is included in these analytics.
  • Hosting Platform: The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

3. Cloud Storage Integration

When you choose to sync your data with cloud storage providers:

  • OneDrive: We access only the specific file you select or create. Your data is stored in your OneDrive account.
  • Google Drive: We use the drive.file and drive.readonly scopes to enable you to browse, select, and save files in your Google Drive. We can only read the list of files to display in the file picker and can only modify files you explicitly select or that we create. We do not access or modify any other files.
  • Local Files: When using local file storage, files remain entirely on your device.

4. What Information We Access

The only information we access includes:

  • Financial data you enter into the app (transactions, budgets, accounts)
  • File names and folder structure when browsing your cloud storage (to help you select files)
  • Content of files you explicitly select or create in cloud storage
  • Your authentication tokens (stored locally) to access cloud storage services

5. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Display your financial data within the app
  • Sync your data to the cloud storage location you choose
  • Generate reports and visualizations based on your data

6. Data Security

  • Your data is encrypted when stored in cloud services (handled by the cloud provider)
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely in your browser
  • No data is transmitted to our servers or third parties

7. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete your data anytime by clearing browser storage or deleting your cloud file
  • Revoke app access through your Google Account or Microsoft Account settings
  • Export your data as a backup file
  • Switch between storage providers at any time

8. Third-Party Services

We integrate with:

  • Google Drive API: Subject to Google API Terms of Service. The app uses the drive.readonly scope which is a sensitive scope. We only use this permission to display your files in the file picker to help you select or create files.
  • Microsoft OneDrive API: Subject to Microsoft API Terms

Important: We never read, modify, or delete files other than the specific Money Tree data file you select or create. The drive.readonly scope is only used to show file names in the picker interface.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
