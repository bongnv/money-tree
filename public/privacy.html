Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 19, 2026

Introduction

Money Tree ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

What Information We Collect

We only collect anonymous analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect:

  • Page views
  • Session duration
  • Referrer information

This data is anonymous, does not use cookies, and does not include any financial information or personal data.

We do not collect your financial data. All financial information you enter (transactions, budgets, accounts, categories) stays on your device or in your cloud storage. We never see, store, or have access to it.

How We Use and Store Your Data

Your data stays on your device. All financial data is stored in your browser's local storage. If you enable cloud sync, your data is transmitted directly from your device to your chosen cloud storage - never through our servers.

Google Drive Integration:

  • We use drive.file scope to read and write your Money Tree data file
  • We use drive.readonly scope to show your files in the picker so you can choose where to save
  • We only access files you explicitly select or that we create
  • All communication uses HTTPS encryption
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely in your browser

OneDrive Integration: We only access the specific file you select or create.

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Your financial information is transmitted only between your device and your chosen cloud storage provider.

Your Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access & Edit: View and modify your data anytime through the app
  • Export: Download your data in JSON format to use with other services
  • Delete: Clear browser storage or delete your cloud storage file to remove all data
  • Disconnect: Remove cloud sync anytime from app settings (doesn't delete the cloud file)
  • Revoke Access: Remove app permissions through Google or Microsoft account settings

Google API Compliance

Money Tree complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements:

  • We use Google Drive API only to store and retrieve your finance data
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not use your data for any purpose beyond app functionality

Third-Party Services

When using cloud storage, you're also subject to: Google, Microsoft, and Cloudflare privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy. Check the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use means you accept any changes.
